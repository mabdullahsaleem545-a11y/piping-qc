    Checking…

    Saudi Aramco SAES, SAEP, SAIC, SATIP, SATR, GI and Schedule Q documents are proprietary and revised often. Clause numbers and values here come from the study deck and public codes. Check each against the current revision held by your project or inspection agency before quoting it. Welding QC is out of scope.

    Flashcards

    Say the answer out loud before you flip. Cards you know move up a box and come back later (1, 2, 4, 8, 16, then 32 days). Cards you miss go back to box 1 and return tomorrow.

    Quiz

    Rounds mix stages on purpose. Mixing is harder than studying one topic at a time, and that is why it sticks. A wrong answer sends the matching flashcard back to box 1.

    Hydrotest pressure, step by step

    Pick a line and see how the test pressure, relief valve and gauge are chosen. The rule is SAES-L-150 §7.1: 90% SMYS hoop stress in the pipe, unless a flange or valve limits it, and never below the B31.3 minimum.

    B31.3 minimum uses St/S = 1 (test at ambient, design below the creep range). Stainless flanges are Group 2.x with lower ratings, so check the B16.5 table for them.

    8 weeks, about 75 minutes a day

    How this page helps you remember

    Last time, reading the notes for three weeks did not stick. Reading feels productive but fades fast. Memory research points to a few habits that make learning last, and each tool on this page is built around one of them.

    A daily routine that uses all of these: quiz first (5 minutes, cold), learn one stage chunk with Recall mode on (25 minutes), flashcards that are due (30 minutes), then explain one point aloud as if to a new trainee (5 minutes). Sleep 7 or more hours, because memories are consolidated during sleep.

    What changed after the review

    I re-checked every point against your study deck (whose values were taken from the text of SAES-L-108, L-109, L-150, L-310, L-350, SAES-A-004, A-007, SAEP-351 and SAEP-1151) and against public ASME and API codes. Aramco standards are proprietary and I could not open the current issues from here, so points marked verify need a check against your agency's current revision.
